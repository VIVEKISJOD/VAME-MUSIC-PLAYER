## Services

- **[LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net)** provides the synced lyrics (free service, no key). Lyrics belong to their respective rights holders. VAME fetches them while you listen and does not store or redistribute them in this repository.
- Song information, audio streams and cover images are loaded from YouTube / YouTube Music while the app runs. No audio or artwork is stored in this repository.
